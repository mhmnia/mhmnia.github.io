# Community profile sources

Research summaries checked against the linked personal or institutional profiles on 9 September 2026. Portraits for twelve members were supplied by the site owner and matched by filename. Existing supervisor/examiner labels are preserved.

## Prof. Valérie Cayol

Develops 3D deformation models to investigate magma storage and transport, volcanic flank motion, and the influence of topography on InSAR observations.

Source: [Prof. Valérie Cayol](https://lmv.uca.fr/cayol-valerie-us/)

## Prof. Pablo J. González

Uses InSAR and mechanical modelling to investigate magma storage and migration, volcano flank instability, and interactions between fluids, rocks and faults.

Source: [Prof. Pablo J. González](https://pablojgonzalez.github.io/)

## Prof. Susanna Ebmeier

Uses satellite observations to study magma storage, volcanic edifice growth and collapse, and interactions between volcanoes, with applications to monitoring and hazards.

Source: [Prof. Susanna Ebmeier](https://environment.leeds.ac.uk/see/staff/1246/dr-susanna-ebmeier)

## Prof. Juliet Biggs

Combines satellite geodesy and machine learning to investigate volcanic unrest, continental rifting and fault deformation, and improve hazard monitoring.

Source: [Prof. Juliet Biggs](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/people/person/Juliet-Biggs-57455bd8-0db3-4a58-a4e0-e89885fd5466/)

## Prof. Matthew E. Pritchard

Investigates surface deformation caused by earthquakes, magma movement, glacier dynamics and human manipulation of underground fluids, including carbon storage and resource extraction.

Source: [Prof. Matthew E. Pritchard](https://pritchard.eas.cornell.edu/)

## Prof. Tim Wright

Develops InSAR observations of strain accumulation, earthquake deformation and magmatic rifting to understand active faults and the growth of continental crust.

Source: [Prof. Tim Wright](https://environment.leeds.ac.uk/see/staff/1613/professor-tim-wright)

## Prof. Paul Segall

Combines geodetic observations and physics-based models to study earthquake nucleation, slow slip, induced seismicity, and magma migration before volcanic eruptions.

Source: [Prof. Paul Segall](https://geophysics.stanford.edu/people/paul-segall)

## Prof. Thomas Walter

Combines satellite radar, drone photogrammetry and field measurements to investigate lava dome growth, volcanic flank instability, and interactions between earthquakes and volcanoes.

Source: [Prof. Thomas Walter](https://www.gfz.de/staff/thomas.walter)

## Prof. Richard Walker

Studies active faults and past earthquakes through landscape analysis, satellite observations and field geology, particularly across Central Asia and the Caucasus.

Source: [Prof. Richard Walker](https://www.environmental-research.ox.ac.uk/people/richard-walker)

## Prof. IJ Hamling

Uses InSAR and geodetic modelling to investigate tectonic and volcanic deformation, including the complex multi-fault rupture of New Zealand’s Kaikōura earthquake.

Source: [Prof. IJ Hamling](https://www.gns.cri.nz/about-us/staff-search/ian-hamling/)

## Prof. Andy Hooper

Develops InSAR time-series methods, including StaMPS, and combines deformation modelling with machine learning to investigate volcanic and tectonic processes.

Source: [Prof. Andy Hooper](https://environment.leeds.ac.uk/see/staff/1334/professor-andy-hooper)

## Prof. Valerio Acocella

Studies volcano–tectonic processes across scales, with research on calderas, Etna and Campi Flegrei, and methods for forecasting eruptions.

Source: [Prof. Valerio Acocella](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Valerio-Acocella-2)

## Prof. Joel Ruch

Investigates interactions between volcanism, faulting and tectonics, including caldera collapse, flank instability and dyke intrusion along rift zones.

Source: [Prof. Joel Ruch](https://www.unige.ch/sciences/terre/en/people/dst/professors/joel-ruch)

## Prof. Raphael Grandin

Studies earthquake processes, volcanic deformation and tectonic–magmatic interactions, with particular interest in Afar, the India–Eurasia collision zone and Chilean subduction.

Source: [Prof. Raphael Grandin](https://www.ipgp.fr/~grandin/Raphael_Grandin_personal_web_page/Home.html)

## Prof. Michael Poland

Uses InSAR and microgravity measurements to track volcanic deformation and subsurface fluid changes, improving understanding of unrest and forecasting of volcanic activity.

Source: [Prof. Michael Poland](https://www.usgs.gov/staff-profiles/michael-poland)

## Prof. Freysteinn Sigmundsson

Uses space geodesy to investigate magma movement, tectonic deformation and crustal responses to changing ice loads, with extensive research on Icelandic volcanoes.

Source: [Prof. Freysteinn Sigmundsson](https://hi.is/staff/fs)
