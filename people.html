People I follow

Researchers whose work shaped how volcano deformation is measured from space, and whose papers I read as they come out. If you are starting in this field and want to know where to look, this is a reasonable place to begin. I will keep adding to the list.

Pablo J. González

Research Reader, IPNA-CSIC, Tenerife, Spain

Volcano and earthquake geodesy, source modelling, and the software the community uses to do it. My doctoral supervisor, and the person who taught me to distrust a signal until the atmosphere has been accounted for.

Their page

Susanna Ebmeier

Associate Professor, University of Leeds, UK

Known for systematic, satellite-wide surveys of volcano deformation rather than single case studies — asking what fraction of the world's volcanoes deform, and what that says about which ones erupt.

Their page

Juliet Biggs

Professor of Earth Sciences, University of Bristol, UK

A central figure in linking satellite geodesy to eruption forecasting, including large statistical studies of how deformation relates to eruption at volcanoes worldwide, and how to use that in hazard assessment.
