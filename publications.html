Peer-reviewed journal articles

4 papers · all first-authored · * denotes corresponding author

  1. Satellite surface deformation survey reveals evidence for active magmatic processes at the Tibesti (Africa) continental intraplate volcanoes

    Mohammadnia, M., Boulesteix, T., Webb, A. A. G., & González, P. J.

    Bulletin of Volcanology, 88, 37 · 2026

    The first satellite geodetic evidence of active magmatic processes in an African continental intraplate volcanic province. Persistent subsidence at Toussidé–Yirrigué and Soborom is attributed to Holocene-emplaced magma reservoirs that are cooling and degassing, supported by thermobarometry, Rhyolite-MELTS modelling and viscoelastic cooling models.

    DOI 10.1007/s00445-026-01954-0 Open access

  2. Spontaneous transient summit uplift at Taftan volcano (Makran subduction arc) imaged using an InSAR common-mode filtering method

    Mohammadnia, M., Yip, M. W., Webb, A. A. G., & González, P. J.

    Geophysical Research Letters, 52(19), e2025GL114853 · 2025

    A 10-month, ~9 cm summit uplift at a volcano with no dated eruption for roughly 700,000 years, resolved after common-mode filtering and modelled to a source 490–630 m beneath the summit. No earthquake preceded it. Widely covered in international media.

    DOI 10.1029/2025GL114853 CMF method Code archive Press coverage Open access

  3. Surface deformation dynamics of Sierra Negra's 2018 eruption: insights from InSAR, optical flow and pixel offset tracking

    Mohammadnia, M.*, Vajedian, S., Mousavi, Z., & Aflaki, M.

    Geophysical Journal International, 240(3), 1773–1789 · 2025

    Three complementary measurement techniques applied to six-day Sentinel-1 pairs across a full eruption cycle. A single one-day ascending–descending offset pair yields the first estimate of subsurface magma transfer rate for this eruption, roughly 60 m per hour.

    DOI 10.1093/gji/ggaf012 Open access

  4. InSAR constraints on the active deformation of salt diapirs in the Kalut basin, central Iran

    Mohammadnia, M., Najafi, M., & Mousavi, Z.

    Tectonophysics, 810, 228860 · 2021

    One of the first InSAR studies to cover an entire diapiric basin rather than a single structure: 137 SLC scenes, 724 interferograms, eight diapirs. Salt rises at up to 8 mm/yr, and walls, stocks, massifs and welds each carry a distinct kinematic signature.

    DOI 10.1016/j.tecto.2021.228860

Software

2 releases · citable, archived

  1. Common-Mode Filtering (CMF) for InSAR time series

    Mohammadnia, M., Yip, M. W., Webb, A. G., & González, P. J.

    Zenodo · v1, January 2025 · GPL-3.0

    A data-driven atmospheric-noise mitigation tool for small-area volcano deformation, designed to slot into MintPy, StaMPS and LiCSBAS workflows. Adopted as the standard filtering step for volcanic-centre time series in Mohammadnia et al. (2026).

    DOI 10.5281/zenodo.14629139 GitHub How it works

  2. Earthquake–Volcano Proximity Explorer

    Mohammadnia, M.

    Zenodo · v1.0.0, August 2026 · GPL-3.0

    A browser-based screening tool that lists every Smithsonian GVP Holocene volcano within a chosen radius of a selected USGS earthquake, with distance, azimuth and eruption history, built to support earthquake–volcano interaction studies.

    DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22067994 GitHub Open the tool
