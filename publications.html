Satellite surface deformation survey reveals evidence for active magmatic processes at the Tibesti continental intraplate volcanoes

Mohammadnia, M., Boulesteix, T., Webb, A. A. G., & González, P. J.

Bulletin of Volcanology, 88, 37 · 2026

The first satellite geodetic evidence of active magmatic processes in an African continental intraplate province. Subsidence at Toussidé–Yirrigué and Soborom traces back to Holocene-emplaced reservoirs that are cooling and degassing, supported by thermobarometry, Rhyolite-MELTS modelling and viscoelastic cooling models.

doi 10.1007/s00445-026-01954-0 Open access

Spontaneous transient summit uplift at Taftan volcano (Makran subduction arc) imaged using an InSAR common-mode filtering method

Mohammadnia, M., Yip, M. W., Webb, A. A. G., & González, P. J.

Geophysical Research Letters, 52(19), e2025GL114853 · 2025

A ten-month, ~9 cm summit uplift at a volcano with no dated eruption for roughly 700,000 years, resolved after common-mode filtering and modelled to a source 490–630 m beneath the summit. No earthquake preceded it. Covered widely in the international press.

doi 10.1029/2025GL114853 The CMF method Code archive Open access

Surface deformation dynamics of Sierra Negra's 2018 eruption: insights from InSAR, optical flow and pixel offset tracking

Mohammadnia, M.*, Vajedian, S., Mousavi, Z., & Aflaki, M.

Geophysical Journal International, 240(3), 1773–1789 · 2025

Three complementary measurement techniques on six-day Sentinel-1 pairs across a full eruption cycle. A single one-day ascending–descending offset pair yields the first estimate of subsurface magma transfer rate for this eruption, roughly 60 m per hour.

doi 10.1093/gji/ggaf012 Open access

InSAR constraints on the active deformation of salt diapirs in the Kalut basin, central Iran

Mohammadnia, M., Najafi, M., & Mousavi, Z.

Tectonophysics, 810, 228860 · 2021

One of the first InSAR studies to cover an entire diapiric basin rather than a single structure: 137 SLC scenes, 724 interferograms, eight diapirs. Salt rises at up to 8 mm/yr, and walls, stocks, massifs and welds each carry a distinct kinematic signature.

doi 10.1016/j.tecto.2021.228860

Common-Mode Filtering (CMF) for InSAR time series

Mohammadnia, M., Yip, M. W., Webb, A. G., & González, P. J.

Zenodo · v1, January 2025 · GPL-3.0

Data-driven atmospheric-noise mitigation for small-area volcano deformation, built to drop into MintPy, StaMPS and LiCSBAS workflows. Now the standard filtering step in the Tibesti study.

Earthquake–Volcano Proximity Explorer

Browser tool that lists every Smithsonian GVP Holocene volcano within a chosen radius of a selected USGS earthquake, then checks the ASF radar archive for tracks that could image it.
